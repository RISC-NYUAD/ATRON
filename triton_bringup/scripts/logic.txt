
Frequency: 500 Hz
Off: 75% Duty Cycle
Forward: 100% Duty Cycle
Reverse: 50% Duty Cycle

Configurations:
Joystick is pressed forward (0 <= linear.x <= 1.5):
- scale both thrusters to PWM between 75% and 100%

Joystick is pressed backward (-1.5 <= linear.x <= 0):
- scale both thrusters to PWM between 50% and 75%

Joystick is pressed to the left(0 <= angular.z <= 1.0):
- scale right thruster between 75% and 100%
- scale left thruster between 50% and 75%

Joystick is pressed to the right (-1.0 <= angular.z <= 0)
- scale right thruster betweeen 50% and 75%
- scale left thruster between 75% and 100%

Do not have mixed considerations, choose only one of the four cases. However, in a lot of cases, both linear.x and angular.z will be nonzero. To decide the priority (if to travel linearly or to rotate), choose the greater value between abs(linear.x)/1.5 and abs(angular.z)/1.0.
